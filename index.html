About

Giverny Kate

Giverny Kate is a nature and wildlife photographer based in Ōtepoti Dunedin, on New Zealand's Otago coast. Her work follows the animals and places close to home: albatross and petrels riding the swell, sea lion pups on the beach, bellbirds in the bush, and the light over Otago's hills.

Genuine photography

Every image here began as a real photograph taken with a camera. Read how photographs are edited, and how any use of AI is disclosed, in the Photography and AI Transparency Policy.

Photography & AI

Our Photography and AI Transparency Policy

Authentic photography. Professional enhancement. Complete transparency.

We believe in the value of authentic photography and believe our customers have the right to know how the images appearing on our products have been created.

Our images originate from genuine photographs captured using conventional cameras. We do not represent artificially generated images as original photography.

Professional photographic enhancement

Our photographs may be professionally enhanced and refined using conventional photographic editing software, including Adobe Photoshop, Lightroom and similar applications.

This may include:

  • Colour correction and tonal adjustments.
  • Brightness, contrast and exposure correction.
  • Cropping, resizing and sharpening.
  • Removal of photographic imperfections.
  • Retouching and restoration.
  • Other conventional photographic enhancement techniques.

These are established photographic post-production techniques and do not constitute AI-generated imagery.

Artificial Intelligence (AI) enhancement

We recognise that AI technology is increasingly being used in modern photographic editing. Where an image has been enhanced, modified or materially altered using AI tools, we will clearly identify that image as AI-enhanced.

This disclosure may apply to AI-assisted image restoration, enhancement, background modification, object removal, image extension or the addition of visual elements. Where applicable, our product descriptions will identify the use of AI so customers can make an informed purchasing decision.

Our commitment

Our commitment is straightforward:

  • Authentic photographic origins.
  • Professional photographic enhancement is permitted.
  • AI-assisted enhancement is disclosed.
  • No artificial imagery is misrepresented as genuine photography.

We believe transparency builds trust and that our customers should always know what they are purchasing.
