About

Giverny Kate

Giverny Kate is a nature and wildlife photographer based in Ōtepoti Dunedin, on New Zealand's Otago coast. Her work follows the animals and places close to home: albatross and petrels riding the swell, sea lion pups on the beach, bellbirds in the bush, and the light over Otago's hills.

Buying a print or gift

Her photographs are printed to order through her Redbubble shop and posted worldwide.
